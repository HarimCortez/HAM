# HAM brand source: Miami Temple SDA Church

Direction from the product owner: HAM's look and feel should follow the church's brand at https://miamitemple.org.

## What we know
- **Name:** Miami Temple Seventh-day Adventist Church
- **Mission line:** "Reaching Up, Reaching Around, Reaching Out"
- **Identity:** multilingual, multicultural, welcoming ("all attire welcome", "you are special"). The site offers Spanish and Russian translations.
- **Logo (horizontal, white version for dark backgrounds):**
  `https://www.miamitemple.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/02/MiamiTemple_HorizWeb_Whitenoshad_Logo.png`
- **Website platform:** WordPress with Slider Revolution. Automated page reading did not expose the colors or font names.

## Still needed (ask the product owner)
- The logo file(s) attached directly: a full-color version and the white version. This workspace cannot download files from miamitemple.org.
- The church's exact brand colors (hex codes) and fonts, if a brand guide exists. If not, ham-ui-designer derives the palette from the attached logo.

## Guidance for ham-ui-designer
- Use the church's colors as HAM's brand accent. Keep neutrals, semantic colors (success/warning/danger) and status colors separate, and check everything for WCAG 2.2 AA contrast.
- Use the logo in the app header and on the requester-facing pages. HAM is a ministry of Miami Temple, so the pages should feel like part of the church.
- Match the welcoming, multicultural tone of the site. V1 is English-only (PRD §75), but layouts should leave room for longer translated text later.
